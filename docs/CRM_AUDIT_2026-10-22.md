# Relay CRM: end-to-end product audit and improvements

Audit date: 22 October 2026. Scope: the local B2B sales and customer-support application, its connected workflows, and comparison with mature commercial and open-source CRMs. This report does not certify deployment or a complete ERP.

Relay now supports a connected internal journey from lead capture by a local public form, a user or CSV, through conversion and follow-up, to a commercial quotation and recorded customer acceptance that can close the deal. Customer context, campaign membership, support records and sales forecasting are connected to the real database. Private saved-view API/storage also remains, although its list controls were removed during presentation cleanup. It is a usable CRM foundation, but it does **not** have all the capabilities of a mature customer-engagement platform: external communications, operational support intake/SLA, advanced customization and a connector ecosystem remain gaps outside this hackathon pass.

This assessment compares documented capabilities, not market-share rankings. Vendor features vary by edition, paid extension and release. GitHub development-branch documentation is not proof that every feature ships in a stable release. Existing ERP proposals in `PLAN.md` were excluded from implemented-feature counts.

## Major connections repaired

**Assigned work can progress without exposing somebody else's customer records.** A sales rep assigned a deal, ticket or task can perform the allowed workflow action while retaining its existing private customer reference. This does not grant access to that customer through record endpoints, lists, search or timelines. New or changed references still require normal record access. See `backend/app/modules/crm/repository.py`, `service.py` and `backend/tests/test_connected_workflow_repairs.py`.

**Pipeline probability changes now reach live opportunities and reports.** Changing an open stage's probability updates its open deals, increments their versions and records the change. Closed outcomes remain protected. Renaming or reordering stages does not silently reset a deal's existing probability override. This connects pipeline settings to the weighted forecast instead of leaving stale deal probabilities behind.

**Email preparation now connects records, templates and saved drafts.** Users can start from a customer record, apply an email template and reopen the persisted draft from that record. Draft retrieval and list filters enforce current tenant/owner/linked-record access. Editing message text preserves omitted relationship fields and validates the final link pair. Supported template variables are `name`, `email` and `company`; unresolved placeholders are called out for review. These changes do not enable sending.

**Date entry preserves the browser's chosen value.** Shared CRM date inputs now keep their native entry value and synchronize on blur, while form submission continues to read the actual field. This addresses date entry/picker values being overwritten during React updates. Existing timestamp-preservation behavior remains important when an editor shows only the date portion of a stored appointment.

**Sales reporting now uses the relevant dates.** `GET /reports/sales` has a dedicated forecast implementation. Open deals use expected close dates; won/lost deals use their actual closing day in Asia/Kolkata. The report distinguishes period totals from overdue/undated attention lists, which cover all accessible open deals in the selected owner/pipeline scope. Existing overview creation-cohort metrics should not be mistaken for this close-date report.

**Quotation acceptance can complete the sales workflow atomically.** The user records the customer's decision and can explicitly choose to mark the linked deal won at the quotation total. The server requires the current quote and deal versions, verifies the customer relationship and writes both outcomes through their owning services in one transaction. A failure after the deal update rolls back the quote decision, deal state/value/version and stage history. Linked customer/deal records cannot be archived or reparented in a way that strands quotation history.

## What the quotation status actually means

`draft` permits commercial edits. `issued` locks the commercial terms and customer snapshot. `accepted` or `declined` records a user-entered customer decision and evidence/reference text. None of those statuses confirms email delivery, a provider-verified signature, invoicing or payment.

Each line uses Decimal quantity/rate arithmetic, rounds its amount to paise, and contributes to the subtotal before the discount is subtracted. Create and transition requests require an idempotency key. Expired offers cannot be issued or accepted. Issued documents have no delete/edit path; changed terms require a new quotation.

The printable popup contains the saved commercial document and can use the browser's print workflow. The popup and its contents were verified; saving a file through a user's native PDF dialog was not automated.

## Architecture and local setup changes

The application remains a modular monolith: Next.js frontend, FastAPI services and real SQLite persistence. The shared CRM repository remains the base for tenant/owner scoping. Commercial and saved-view modules own their new tables; commercial changes to deals go through the CRM service.

| Addition | Main files | Schema change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quotations | `backend/app/modules/commercial/`; `frontend/src/components/quotations.tsx` and its CSS module | `backend/local_migrations/0006_quotations.sql`: quotations, lines and quotation events |
| Sales forecasting | `backend/app/modules/crm/reporting.py`, `reporting_router.py`; `frontend/src/components/forecast-panel.tsx` | No new table; queries current deals/stages |
| Private saved views | `backend/app/modules/views/`; `frontend/src/components/saved-views.tsx` | `backend/local_migrations/0007_saved_views.sql` |
| Connected draft preparation | `backend/app/modules/engagement/`; `engagement-template-picker.tsx`, `engagement-record-drafts.tsx`, shared CRM editor | Existing email draft/template tables |

Restart the local API after these additions so its numbered migration runner applies `0006` through `0010` and loads the new routes. Applied migrations were not edited. Generated OpenAPI/TypeScript contracts include the new response schemas; quotation UI imports the generated types. No new runtime dependency is required for these product additions.

No external provider credentials are needed for the implemented local workflows. Gmail sending/synchronization remains deliberately deferred, and the connector code itself is absent: supplying OAuth credentials alone would not enable it.

## Remaining product roadmap, in priority order

1. **Future external communication.** Local public lead forms, validation/deduplication, routing and follow-up are now implemented. When the explicitly deferred integration scope is resumed, implement one real mailbox/calendar adapter with inbound threading, outbound status evidence and reconnect/error handling. Frappe documents forms and assignment rules; Pipedrive documents connected mailbox synchronization. [Frappe setup](https://docs.frappe.io/crm/setup-guide/your-first-steps-with-frappe-crm), [assignment rules](https://docs.frappe.io/crm/assignment-rule), [Pipedrive email sync](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/email-sync).

2. **Turn internal support records into a working customer service loop.** Connect an intake channel, preserve customer context through responses, and add first-response/resolution targets with working hours and escalation. A customer portal and knowledge base can follow according to support volume. [HubSpot's SLA documentation](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/help-desk/set-sla-goals-in-help-desk) is a concrete reference for response and resolution goals; it also makes the subscription boundaries explicit.

3. **Future automation depth.** Durable ordered delays, owner assignment, retries and run visibility are now implemented. Branching, recurring schedules and arbitrary field updates remain future scope; preserve current idempotency and permissions. [Frappe automation actions](https://docs.frappe.io/crm/automations/actions) and [SuiteCRM workflows](https://docs.suitecrm.com/admin/administration-panel/workflow/) provide practical comparisons. A task due date is not a scheduled workflow execution.

4. **Extend the sales agreement into fulfillment and retention.** Build a reusable product/service catalog, accepted quote revisions or supersession, and explicit onboarding/renewal tasks or a verified external handoff. Integrate order/accounting systems when needed instead of claiming that a won deal is cash collected. [Dynamics Sales](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/sales/overview) describes the broader lead-to-order journey; [EspoCRM quotations](https://docs.espocrm.com/user-guide/quotes/) illustrates the commercial handoff in its Sales Pack extension.

5. **Make the CRM fit more teams.** Typed custom fields are implemented. Add shared views, safe bulk actions and contact/company deduplication before attempting an unrestricted custom-object platform. Existing private preset storage remains, but saved-view controls are not exposed in the current UI. [Twenty's user guide](https://docs.twenty.com/user-guide/introduction), [Frappe CRM](https://github.com/frappe/crm/blob/develop/README.md) and [EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm/blob/master/README.md) document comparable extensibility.

6. **Build deeper management reporting and integrations.** Add quota/target attainment, historical forecast snapshots, stage conversion/velocity and configurable reports; then scoped API credentials, signed webhooks and observable connector retries. The new forecast solves the close-date reporting gap but does not cover these capabilities. [Salesforce Sales Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/cloud/), [Pipedrive forecast](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/insights-reports-revenue-forecast), [SuiteCRM reports](https://docs.suitecrm.com/user/insights/reports/) and [scheduled reports](https://docs.suitecrm.com/user/insights/scheduled-reports/) provide the comparison baseline.

Predictive AI, complex CPQ, territory management, SSO/SCIM, offline mobile and advanced omnichannel operation depend on the target customer segment; they are not universal hackathon requirements. Stock valuation, payroll, manufacturing, tax ledgers, GST filing and full procure-to-pay remain separate ERP scope.
